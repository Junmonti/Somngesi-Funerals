Our story

A commitment shaped by years of service.

Somngesi Funerals was established on 6 January 2020 by founder Xhanti Somngesi. The business grew from a desire to bring personal experience, compassion and fairness to families during difficult times.

Before opening the company, Xhanti worked weekends at Uitenhage Funeral Home from the early 2000s, gaining approximately 15–19 years of experience in the funeral industry.

The first Somngesi Funerals office was located at 153 Mabandla Road. In 2022, the business moved to its current office at 89 Mabandla Road, KwaNobuhle, Uitenhage.
